# Sales-performance-Analysis-Dashboard---Google-sheets
Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

# PROJECT OVERVIEW
I analyzed a sales dataset using Google sheets to understand sales performance,and performance across different states for a period of 3 months .
The project focused on transforming raw business data into clear insights that could support better sales and business decisions.
 
# BUSINESS PROBLEM
The business needed a simple way to understand:
- Overall sales and order performance for a 3 months window.
- Which states were performing best.
- Which products were performing well.
- Which customers contributed most to sales.
- Where management could focus its sales and customer-retention efforts.
- Which state should management consider in opening a physical office 

# MY APPROACH
I followed a structured data analysis process:

- Data Cleaaning- Reviewed and organized the raw dataset to make it suitable for analysis
- Data Preparation- Structured the data for calculations,summaries, and visualization.
- Exploratory Analysis- Examined sales, customers, orders, products and state level performance
- KPI Development- Created key performance indicators for quick business monitoring
- Data Visualization- Built charts to make important patterns easier to understand
- Dashboard Development- Combined the analysis into a single business dashboard
- Business Interpretation- Translated the results into information that can support decision-making.

# KEY ANALYSIS

# DATA PRIVACY AND PII PROTECTION

Because customer-related information was included in the original dataset, i treated privacy as an important part of the analysis.
Measures taken included:
- Removed or excluded direct personal identifiers from the dataset
- Did not expose customer names,phone numbers,email addresses,home addresses or other direct identifiers
- Used non-identifying customer IDs where customer-level analysis was necessary
- Ensured that the dashboard and screenshots shared publicly did not expose personal customer information.
- The portfolio version contains anonymized/aggregated information intended for demonstration purposes
- No personally identifiable information (PII) was intentionally published in the portfolio

# KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Based on the analysis, the following patterns were identified:
- Lagos was the highest-performing state
lagos recorded the strongest overall sales performance among the states analyzed. This suggests that lagos is an important market for the business and may
deserve continued sales and customer-engagement attention

- Sugar Tea was the besting-performing  product
Sugar tea recorded the strongest product performance in the dataset.
This indicates strong customer demand and makes it a potential priority product for stock planning and promotional activities.

-Customer ID 1103 was the highest-value customer
Customer ID 1103 recorded the highest level of patronage among the customers analyzed. This customer represents an important customer 
segment for retention and relationship management

- Product preferences differed by state
  The analysis showed differences in product demand across locations. In particular, SUGAR tea and INFECTION tea were frequently ordered by customers in
  Abia State, While products including ULCER tea, TEA for men, B.P tea also showed notable demand patterns.
  This indicates that customer preferences may vary by location and that a single sales strategy may not work equally well across all markets.

  # BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on these findings, i would recommend that the business:
- Strengthen the lagos market.
  continue monitoring lagos performance and consider targeted promotions, customer engagement,and stock availability because it is currently the strongest-performing state.
  And it is also considered in opening a physical office
- Prioritize high-demand products.
  Maintain adequate stock of sugar tea and other products showing strong demand to reduce the possibility of missed sales opportunities
- Develop customer-retention strategies.
  Customers with high patronage, such as customer ID 1103, could be targeted with loyalty programs, personalized offers,early access to promotions, or other retention initiatives
- Monitor product trends over time.
  The business should continue tracking product and state performance over time to determine whether these patterns remain consistent and to identify emerging opportunities
